# CHURN REDUCTION INITIATIVE

## Executive Summary

### Situation
Customer churn is our leading revenue loss driver, costing $2,000,000 annually. Our current annual churn rate stands at 7%, significantly exceeding the SaaS industry benchmark of 4%. Following a comprehensive root-cause analysis, we identified support response delay as the primary operational bottleneck and formulated a high-ROI intervention plan.

### Key Findings
- **Response Speed Controls Churn**: Customers receiving support in under 2 hours experience 3% churn, whereas customers waiting over 24 hours experience 12% churn (a 4-fold increase).
- **Operational Bottleneck**: Our current support team averages a 6-hour first-response time, pushing at-risk accounts beyond the satisfaction threshold.
- **High-Value Account Sensitivity**: High-value accounts (>$10,000 annual spend) churn at 15% when support is delayed, exposing our highest-margin revenue stream.

### Business Risks
- **Revenue at Risk**: Doing nothing perpetuates a $2,000,000 annual revenue loss, eroding enterprise customer lifetime value.
- **Competitive Vulnerability**: Competitors with faster support SLAs actively target our dissatisfied accounts.
- **Operational Degradation**: Support ticket volume grew 40% year-over-year while response times degraded. Without intervention, annual churn will escalate beyond 9%.

### Recommendations
1. **Hire 2 Support Engineers** (Cost: $200,000/year): Reduces average response time from 6 hours to under 2 hours. Recovers $400,000 in annual recurring revenue (2x ROI in year 1).
2. **Enforce 2-Hour Response SLA** (Cost: $0): Establishes formal response targets and daily dashboard tracking to drive team accountability by Jan 1.
3. **Prioritize High-Value Accounts** (Cost: $50,000 engineering): Deploys automated priority routing for >$10,000/year accounts by Feb 1, cutting high-value churn by 50%.

### Decision Needed
Approve $200,000 hiring budget and $50,000 engineering allocation by Dec 15 to secure $400,000 in first-year revenue recovery.
